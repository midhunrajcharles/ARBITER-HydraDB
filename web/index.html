Arbiter.
enterprise questions, answered by traversal

The top result looks right. It's from the wrong release.

Ask who worked on the previous release and search hands back a market research report that matches every word of the question — and belongs to the release before it, or to a different product altogether. It reads as correct, so nobody checks. Arbiter resolves the question by traversal instead: release ordering → the artifacts that belong to those releases → the people who authored them → filtered by a role edge. Every answer below shows the query that produced it and every artifact it landed on.
